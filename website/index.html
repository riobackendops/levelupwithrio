FAQ

Questions people ask before booking.

How much does it cost?

Website in a Week is a flat project price, quoted after a short conversation, so you know the exact number before we start. Websites start at $999, and the price rises with the scope. Tell me what you’re building and I’ll send a clear quote.

When does the seven days start?

When your onboarding checklist is complete. The seven business days are intensive and hands-on, so the clock starts when I have the logins, brand files, photos, and information I need. Send it all in one go and your date holds.

Do you write the copy too?

Yes. Page copy is part of the build, and you approve every word.

What if I don’t have a logo or brand yet?

Then we start with the visual direction. You don’t need to arrive polished. I do need real photos and real information about your offers and customers, because that’s what makes the site sound like you.

Could something go live that I haven’t seen?

No. Nothing publishes without your approval.

What happens after the seven days?

You get a free support week for minor fixes and small tweaks, and I’ll run the quality checks with you. After that, you own everything and it’s yours to run. New pages or features are a new piece of work, and I’ll quote them properly.

Who owns the website?

You do. Source files, logins, and assets are yours at the end of the project.

So when do we start?

A free 15-minute call, or a simple chat, is the whole commitment.
